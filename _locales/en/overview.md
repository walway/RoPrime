We are focused on improving experience of Roblox website.

Roblox is known for very bad website experience, but RoPrime fixes that. Just by enabling icon-only sidebar you can feel the difference.

RoPrime is absolutely free and was the answer to Roblox's default sidebar, which is really bad.

Some features from the extension:
🎨 Roblox’s design style
📐 Fully customizable Sidebar
👥 Friend Styling Reimagined
👨 Profile Redesign with Avatar Preview at the center instead of the top of the page
✏️ Rename wording (Communities/Groups, Marketplace/Catalog, Charts/Discover, Experiences/Games)
🌽 More rounded corners
🎛️ Custom CSS
🔄 Sync settings easily
🌓 Light and dark theme support for Normal, Select and Kids Roblox
🌎 Multilanguage

And more!

RoPrime redesigns old Roblox design, while keeping newer ones and implementing new features and fixing what Roblox didn't. You can even make anything with Custom CSS! We are trying to add more features or provide fixes very fast. If we dont have your language, we have easy to understand step-by-step translation guide in our open-source repository.

The project is fully open-source, there is no trackers, and safe. We love our project and do not collecting any data.

By making this project I'm learning something new and how to make anything I want, and how to upgrade user's experience. This is my first project and I'm trying to keep it as main as much as possible.
