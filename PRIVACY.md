# RoPrime Privacy Policy

## Summary

RoPrime is a privacy-oriented browser extension that does not store, log, track, monetize or analyze any personal information including browsing activity and IP addresses. Although some of our legacy features required storing public numerical Roblox User IDs, these features are permanently disabled and users can request deletion of their ID via email.


## Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how we at RoPrime Chrome Extension ("RoPrime") use and protect your personal information that is provided to us or obtained/generated by us in connection with your use of our extension services (the "Services"). In this Privacy Policy 'we', 'us' and 'our' refer to RoPrime, and 'you' refers to you, the user of the Services.

We operate with complete transparency with regards to our architectural approach and how we protect your digital rights around the globe, including United States, the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and internationally.

### 1. Global Core Privacy Principles

Our global privacy philosophy revolves around data minimalism and privacy-by-design, matching the best digital practices around the world. The safest way to keep your data secure is to not collect it in the first place.

* **Monetization Architecture:** We do not monetize our product usage. We do not use your data to track, profile you, and serve personalized advertisements to you.
* **Storage Minimization:** We only store the bare minimum amount of technical information necessary to ensure the proper functioning of the extension on your machine.
* **Zero Tracking:** We do not track, monitor or record your browsing activity, tabs, and opened web pages.
* **Privacy by Design:** Every single feature implemented in RoPrime was built in a way to process all your data locally on your computer whenever technically possible.

### 2. What Personal Data We Process Worldwide

As we do not require you to create an account or sign up for using RoPrime, our operational data footprint is zero for the majority of our features.

#### A. Information You Do Not Provide
* **Identity Data:** We do not collect your real name, biological gender, physical address or date of birth.
* **Contact Data:** We do not ask for or collect your email address or phone number to install and operate the extension.
* **Financial Data:** We do not process any billing or transaction history or credit card numbers.

#### B. Technical Data and Browsing Activity
* **Browsing Logs:** We do not monitor, log or store the URLs of visited websites, your search queries or browser telemetry.
* **Network Identifiers:** We do not log your IP address, ISP and device hardware fingerprint.
* **Cookies &amp; Trackers:** We do not use any cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics software (for example, Google Analytics) in our extension.

### 3. Legacy Cosmetic Data &amp; Roblox User IDs

We offered several cosmetic options in our extensions, namely **Profile Effects** and **Profile Picture Effects**, requiring linking settings of the extension to the profile on the platform.

#### A. What is a Roblox User ID?
The Roblox User ID is a public and unique sequence of numbers which is generated automatically by Roblox platform when you create an account. This is a purely technical piece of data, used to identify public profile on the Roblox platform. 
* It does not contain, encode, or disclose any personal information in the real world.
* It cannot be used by us to discover your real name, email, phone number, credentials or physical location.

#### B. Discontinuation of Cosmetic Features
The above-mentioned cosmetic features have been fully discontinued and disabled.
* RoPrime has been updated in such a way that it will no longer request any User IDs.
* The extension will no longer associate, process or transmit new Roblox User IDs to any of our servers.

#### C. Database Retention Policy
The existing public Roblox User IDs, which we collected in the course of providing cosmetic features mentioned above, still rest in our legacy database.
* **State of Data:** The aforementioned legacy database is completely static, we do not add any new profiles, entries or network connections to it.
* **Purpose:** We keep this data for maintaining legacy back-end consistency and avoiding error logs on legacy configurations. 
* **No Profiling:** These IDs are not cross-referenced, analyzed or associated with any external identity in any way.

#### D. Immediate Deletion Rights
You are free to delete your legacy data footprints. If you wish to have your legacy Roblox User ID deleted from our database records, you can send a direct request at any time. We will delete the specific numerical record completely from our storage infrastructure within a reasonable period of time.

### 4. Data Security &amp; Technical Infrastructure

We take the security of our technical architecture very seriously. Despite not collecting sensitive personal information, we use industry-standard data security measures for our operational servers.

* **Access Control:** Access to our legacy database is strictly restricted to our backend infrastructure. There are no access permissions granted to any third parties.
* **Data Transmission:** Any connection between the extension client and our legacy systems is completely encrypted through transport security layers.
* **Server Maintenance:** We regularly perform audits on our hosting setups in order to ensure they are protected against unauthorized changes, technical exploits and data leaks.
* **Breach Notification:** In case of a very unlikely security breach of our legacy infrastructure, we will post an announcement details page in order to notify our community. Although there is no sensitive, real-world personal information in our legacy database, we strive to be transparent in any situation involving our community.

### 5. Third-Party Websites and Services

When you use RoPrime, you interact with third-party web environments — primarily the Roblox platform and the Google Chrome Web Store environment.

* **Roblox Environment:** RoPrime interacts with public portions of the Roblox website to provide cosmetic modifications. However, we do not control, modify, or take any responsibility for privacy practices, cookies and data collections performed by Roblox itself.
* **Chrome Web Store:** Google controls distribution, installation and permission management of the extension. Google collects telemetry about extension performance as per its privacy policy. We recommend that you read Google's policy for further context.
* **External Links:** If any link from our documentation or settings leads you to an external website, we are not responsible for the protection of privacy or the content of the external domains.

### 6. United States Regional Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA &amp; State Laws)

For the residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other US states with active privacy legislation:

* **No Sale or Sharing of Data:** We do not "sell" your personal information and we do not "share" your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
* **Shine the Light &amp; CPRA:** We do not maintain or disclose consumer profiles to third parties for direct marketing purposes.
* **Right to Know and Delete:** As your historical Roblox User ID is stored statically from historical features, you have a right to request disclosure or deletion of this data by contacting us via our dedicated email support channel.

### 7. United Kingdom &amp; European Union Compliance (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

For users accessing the Services from the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area (EEA):

* **Data Controller:** For the purpose of the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), processing of historical, public Roblox User IDs is limited to maintenance of operational stability of legacy software only.
* **Legal Basis for Processing:** Our legal basis for retention of historical Roblox User IDs is our **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in maintaining stability of the software and its backward compatibility for existing users.
* **Your Data Subject Rights:** Pursuant to GDPR framework, you have the following rights:
  * **Right of Access:** You may request information whether your historical Roblox User ID is present in our database.
  * **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You may request the complete and permanent deletion of your Roblox User ID from our servers.
  * **Right to Object:** You may object to our legitimate interest processing of your public ID at any time.

### 8. International Jurisdictions &amp; Global Compliance

We strive to respect privacy frameworks across the globe. This includes, but is not limited to, compliance frameworks such as Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act 1988, Brazil's LGPD, and Japan's APPI.

* **Global Minimization Policy:** Our extension architecture inherently prevents us from collecting any private consumer identifiers (tracking logs, location coordinates or real identity profiles), therefore our operations are in compliance with international regulatory requirements of data minimization and security.
* **Cross-Border Transfers:** Any data processing or storage takes place on secure servers. Using our extension implies that public technical identifiers (Roblox User IDs) captured historically are safely stored regardless of geographical location of the server infrastructure.

### 9. Children’s Privacy Protection (COPPA &amp; Age Legislation)

Considering the nature of browser extensions targeting gaming platforms, we are keenly aware of the need to protect young consumers globally, including regulations such as COPPA (US) and the Age Appropriate Design Code (UK).

* **Zero Information Harvesting:** We do not collect, harvest, or request any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 within the European Economic Area and UK).
* **Safe Architecture:** Our extension does not collect personal user accounts and has permanently discontinued any kind of database tracking. Therefore, it provides safe, passive user experience and does not create child user profiles.
* **Parental Controls:** If a parent/legal guardian discovers that their child's public Roblox User ID is sitting in our cosmetic legacy database and wishes it to be deleted, they can request it via our contact channel.
